Statements that are not first-order logic over arbitrary sets: each goes to
the algorithm made for it in the mathematical engine, and its result to the
independent checker.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$
\item $\displaystyle \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid x^{2} - 5x + 6 \le 0\}$
\item $\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx}\left( x^{3} \sin x \right)$
\item $\displaystyle \det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = -2$
\item $\displaystyle (A \subseteq S \land B \subseteq S \land C \subseteq S \land A \subseteq B \land B \subseteq C) \implies A \subseteq C$
\item $\displaystyle \forall x \in \mathbb{R}\ \left(x^{2} + 1 > 0\right)$
\end{enumerate}
